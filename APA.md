# APA Referencing Style (7th Edition) Cheat Sheet

> For student assignments and research papers  
> Theme examples: blockchain, digital identity, DeFi, carbon markets, and sustainability

---

## 1. Basic Idea

APA style uses two connected parts:

1. **In-text citation**: a short citation inside your paragraph.
2. **Reference list entry**: the full source information at the end of your paper.

Example:

```text
Blockchain can reduce reliance on central intermediaries in some transaction systems (Nakamoto, 2008).
```

Reference list:

```text
Nakamoto, S. (2008). Bitcoin: A peer-to-peer electronic cash system. https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf
```

中文說明：  
APA 引用包含「文中引用」與「文末參考文獻」。文中引用簡短，文末提供完整資料。

---

## 2. In-Text Citation Format

### 2.1 Parenthetical Citation

Use this when the citation appears at the end of a sentence.

```text
Smart contracts can automate certain contractual processes on blockchain platforms (Buterin, 2014).
```

Format:

```text
(Author, Year)
```

---

### 2.2 Narrative Citation

Use this when the author's name is part of the sentence.

```text
Buterin (2014) introduced Ethereum as a blockchain platform supporting smart contracts.
```

Format:

```text
Author (Year)
```

---

## 3. Number of Authors

### 3.1 One Author

```text
(Nakamoto, 2008)
```

```text
Nakamoto (2008) proposed Bitcoin as a peer-to-peer electronic cash system.
```

---

### 3.2 Two Authors

Use `&` inside parentheses.

```text
(Allen & Overy, 2022)
```

Use `and` in narrative writing.

```text
Allen and Overy (2022) discussed legal issues related to digital assets.
```

---

### 3.3 Three or More Authors

Use `et al.` from the first citation.

```text
(Tapscott et al., 2016)
```

```text
Tapscott et al. (2016) argued that blockchain could transform business and society.
```

中文說明：  
三位以上作者，APA 7th 通常從第一次引用就使用 `et al.`。

---

## 4. Direct Quotation

When quoting directly, include the page number if available.

```text
Blockchain is described as a system that enables transactions without relying on a trusted third party (Nakamoto, 2008, p. 1).
```

Format:

```text
(Author, Year, p. PageNumber)
```

For webpages without page numbers, use paragraph numbers when useful:

```text
(Author, Year, para. 4)
```

---

## 5. Reference List: General Rules

At the end of the paper, create a section titled:

```markdown
## References
```

Rules:

- Arrange entries alphabetically by the first author's surname.
- Use hanging indentation in formal documents.
- Use sentence case for article and book titles.
- Use title case for journal names.
- Italicize book titles and journal titles.
- Include DOI or URL when available.

中文說明：  
Reference list 要依作者姓氏排序。文章標題通常只大寫第一個字與專有名詞；期刊名稱則保留標題式大寫。

## 6. Common Reference Types

## 6.1 Journal Article

Format:

```text
Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of the article. Title of the Journal, volume(issue), page–page. https://doi.org/xxxxx
```

Example:

```text
Beck, R., Müller-Bloch, C., & King, J. L. (2018). Governance in the blockchain economy: A framework and research agenda. Journal of the Association for Information Systems, 19(10), 1020–1034. https://doi.org/10.17705/1jais.00518
```

---

## 6.2 Book

Format:

```text
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of the book. Publisher.
```

Example:

```text
Tapscott, D., & Tapscott, A. (2016). Blockchain revolution: How the technology behind Bitcoin is changing money, business, and the world. Portfolio.
```

---

## 6.3 Edited Book Chapter

Format:

```text
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor (Ed.), Title of book (pp. xx–xx). Publisher.
```

Example:

```text
Davidson, S., De Filippi, P., & Potts, J. (2018). Blockchains and the economic institutions of capitalism. In J. Potts (Ed.), The new technologies of freedom (pp. 183–209). Springer.
```

---

## 6.4 Conference Paper

Format:

```text
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of paper. In Proceedings of the Conference Name (pp. xx–xx). Publisher. https://doi.org/xxxxx
```

Example:

```text
Kosba, A., Miller, A., Shi, E., Wen, Z., & Papamanthou, C. (2016). Hawk: The blockchain model of cryptography and privacy-preserving smart contracts. In 2016 IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (pp. 839–858). IEEE. https://doi.org/10.1109/SP.2016.55
```

---

## 6.5 Website or Webpage

Format:

```text
Author, A. A. or Organization Name. (Year, Month Day). Title of webpage. Website Name. URL
```

Example:

```text
Ethereum Foundation. (2024). Introduction to smart contracts. Ethereum.org. https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/smart-contracts/
```

If the author and website name are the same, omit the website name:

```text
World Economic Forum. (2023). Blockchain for scaling climate action. https://www.weforum.org/
```

---

## 6.6 White Paper

Format:

```text
Author, A. A. or Organization Name. (Year). Title of white paper. Publisher or Organization. URL
```

Example:

```text
Nakamoto, S. (2008). Bitcoin: A peer-to-peer electronic cash system. https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf
```

Example:

```text
Buterin, V. (2014). Ethereum: A next-generation smart contract and decentralized application platform. https://ethereum.org/en/whitepaper/
```

---

## 6.7 Report by an Organization

Format:

```text
Organization Name. (Year). Title of report. Publisher. URL
```

Example:

```text
Bank for International Settlements. (2023). The crypto ecosystem: Key elements and risks. Bank for International Settlements. https://www.bis.org/
```

---

## 6.8 Dataset

Format:

```text
Author, A. A. or Organization Name. (Year). Title of dataset (Version number) [Data set]. Publisher. URL
```

Example:

```text
Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance. (2023). Cambridge Bitcoin Electricity Consumption Index [Data set]. University of Cambridge. https://ccaf.io/cbnsi/cbeci
```

---

## 6.9 Software or Code Repository

Format:

```text
Author, A. A. or Organization Name. (Year). Title of software or repository (Version number) [Computer software]. Publisher or Platform. URL
```

Example:

```text
Hyperledger Foundation. (2024). Hyperledger Fabric [Computer software]. GitHub. https://github.com/hyperledger/fabric
```

Example:

```text
OpenZeppelin. (2024). OpenZeppelin Contracts [Computer software]. GitHub. https://github.com/OpenZeppelin/openzeppelin-contracts
```

---

## 6.10 GitHub Repository

For a GitHub repository, include the organization or author, year, repository title, software description, platform, and URL.

```text
Uniswap. (2024). v3-core [Computer software]. GitHub. https://github.com/Uniswap/v3-core
```

In-text citation:

```text
(Uniswap, 2024)
```

---

## 6.11 AI Tool or Large Language Model

Format:

```text
Author or Organization. (Year). Name of tool (Version or model information) [Large language model]. URL
```

Example:

```text
OpenAI. (2026). ChatGPT (GPT-5.5) [Large language model]. https://chat.openai.com/
```

In-text citation:

```text
(OpenAI, 2026)
```

中文說明：  
如果學生使用 AI 工具協助寫作、翻譯、程式除錯或資料整理，應依教師規定揭露使用方式。APA citation 不等於學術上一定允許使用；課程規則仍優先。

## 7. Examples of In-Text Citations

### Example 1: Blockchain White Paper

```text
Bitcoin introduced a peer-to-peer payment system that does not require a central financial intermediary (Nakamoto, 2008).
```

### Example 2: Smart Contracts

```text
Buterin (2014) proposed Ethereum as a programmable blockchain platform for decentralized applications.
```

### Example 3: Governance

```text
Blockchain governance involves both technical decision-making and institutional coordination (Beck et al., 2018).
```

### Example 4: Sustainability

```text
Blockchain-based carbon markets may improve transparency, but they also raise questions about verification, accountability, and environmental impact (World Economic Forum, 2023).
```

## 9. Quick Checklist for Students

Before submission, check the following:

- [ ] Every in-text citation has a matching reference list entry.
- [ ] Every reference list entry is cited in the text.
- [ ] Author names are spelled consistently.
- [ ] Publication years are correct.
- [ ] Journal titles and volume numbers are italicized in formal formatting.
- [ ] DOIs and URLs are included when available.
- [ ] Sources are credible and appropriate for academic writing.
- [ ] AI-generated content, if used, is disclosed according to course policy.

## 10. Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Missing Year

Incorrect:

```text
(Nakamoto)
```

Correct:

```text
(Nakamoto, 2008)
```

---

### Mistake 2: Using Full First Names in In-Text Citations

Incorrect:

```text
(Satoshi Nakamoto, 2008)
```

Correct:

```text
(Nakamoto, 2008)
```

---

### Mistake 3: Forgetting the Reference List

Incorrect:

```text
Blockchain supports decentralized transactions (Nakamoto, 2008).
```

But no reference list entry is provided.

Correct:

```text
Nakamoto, S. (2008). Bitcoin: A peer-to-peer electronic cash system. https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf
```

---

### Mistake 4: Treating URLs as Full Citations

Incorrect:

```text
https://ethereum.org/en/whitepaper/
```

Correct:

```text
Buterin, V. (2014). Ethereum: A next-generation smart contract and decentralized application platform. https://ethereum.org/en/whitepaper/
```

## 11. Suggested Citation Pattern

When writing academic paragraphs, avoid simply placing citations at the end of every sentence. Instead, integrate sources into your argument.

Weak version:

```text
Blockchain is decentralized (Nakamoto, 2008). Ethereum supports smart contracts (Buterin, 2014). Blockchain governance is complex (Beck et al., 2018).
```

Better version:

```text
Early blockchain systems such as Bitcoin focused on peer-to-peer value transfer without a central intermediary (Nakamoto, 2008). Ethereum extended this model by introducing a programmable blockchain platform for decentralized applications and smart contracts (Buterin, 2014). As blockchain systems became more complex, governance became not only a technical issue but also an institutional challenge (Beck et al., 2018).
```

---

## 12. Final Advice

APA citation is not just a formatting requirement. It helps readers understand:

- where your ideas come from;
- how your argument is supported;
- whether your evidence is credible;
- how your work connects to existing research.

Good citation practice is part of good academic thinking.
