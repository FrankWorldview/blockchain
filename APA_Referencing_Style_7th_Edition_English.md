# APA Referencing Style (7th Edition)

## Quick Guide for Students

### What is APA Style?

APA (American Psychological Association) style is one of the most widely used citation styles in academic writing. It consists of:

1. **In-text citations** – citations within the body of your paper.
2. **Reference list** – complete bibliographic information at the end of the paper.

---

## In-Text Citations

### One Author

**Parenthetical citation**

```text
Blockchain can improve transparency in financial transactions (Nakamoto, 2008).
```

**Narrative citation**

```text
Nakamoto (2008) proposed Bitcoin as a peer-to-peer electronic cash system.
```

### Two Authors

```text
(Allen & Overy, 2022)
```

```text
Allen and Overy (2022) discussed legal challenges related to digital assets.
```

### Three or More Authors

```text
(Tapscott et al., 2016)
```

---

## Direct Quotations

```text
(Nakamoto, 2008, p. 1)
```

For webpages without page numbers:

```text
(World Economic Forum, 2023, para. 4)
```

## Reference List Examples

### Journal Article

```text
Beck, R., Müller-Bloch, C., & King, J. L. (2018). Governance in the blockchain economy: A framework and research agenda. Journal of the Association for Information Systems, 19(10), 1020–1034. https://doi.org/10.17705/1jais.00518
```

### Book

```text
Tapscott, D., & Tapscott, A. (2016). Blockchain revolution: How the technology behind Bitcoin is changing money, business, and the world. Portfolio.
```

### Conference Paper

```text
Kosba, A., Miller, A., Shi, E., Wen, Z., & Papamanthou, C. (2016). Hawk: The blockchain model of cryptography and privacy-preserving smart contracts. In 2016 IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy (pp. 839–858). IEEE.
```

### Website

```text
Ethereum Foundation. (2024). Introduction to smart contracts. Ethereum.org. https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/smart-contracts/
```

### Report

```text
Bank for International Settlements. (2023). The crypto ecosystem: Key elements and risks. Bank for International Settlements.
```

### Dataset

```text
Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance. (2023). Cambridge Bitcoin Electricity Consumption Index [Data set]. University of Cambridge.
```

### Software / GitHub Repository

```text
Hyperledger Foundation. (2024). Hyperledger Fabric [Computer software]. GitHub. https://github.com/hyperledger/fabric
```

## Common Mistakes

- Missing publication year.
- Missing reference list entry.
- Inconsistent author names.
- Using URLs as citations.
- Citing sources that do not appear in the reference list.

---

## Final Checklist

- Every in-text citation appears in the reference list.
- Every reference appears in the text.
- Authors and years are correct.
- DOI or URL is included when available.
- References are arranged alphabetically.
